STAY SAFE

Smart Habits for Safer Medicines

A few simple checks can help you stay informed and avoid suspicious or unsafe medicines.

✓

Check Before You Use

Always verify important medicine details before taking a medicine.

◉

Check Packaging

Look for damaged packaging, unusual printing or missing information.

!

Check Expiry Date

Never use a medicine after its expiry date unless advised by a qualified professional.

+

Buy From Trusted Sources

Purchase medicines from licensed pharmacies and trusted healthcare providers.

⌕

Verify Suspicious Medicines

If something looks unusual, verify the medicine before using it.

♡

Ask a Professional

When in doubt, consult a doctor or qualified pharmacist before taking the medicine.

!

Important

Medi-Sure is designed to provide medicine information and verification assistance. It does not replace professional medical advice.
